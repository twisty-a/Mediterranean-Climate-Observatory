01 · The Problem

Why finding a real climate trend is harder than drawing a line

Standard dashboards drop a single straight line onto a noisy chart and call it a day. In Earth science, that routinely misleads students and coastal planners.

Trap 1 · Short Windows

Noise hides slow signals

A 15-year slice of satellite sea temperature (1982–1998) looks statistically flat even inside a basin warming rapidly over 44 years.

Trap 2 · Memory

Autocorrelation fools OLS

Warm ocean years tend to follow warm ocean years (lag-1 r₁ > 0.5). Plain Student's t-tests treat every year as independent and overstate certainty.

Trap 3 · Opposing Regimes

One driver, opposite slopes

Across the Mediterranean, coastal rainfall trends upward at Alexandria (+44.7 mm/dec) while Athens and Barcelona slope downward — yet none pass robust multiple-testing checks.

03 · Statistical Engine

Every window cross-examined by four tests

Inside js/stats.js, our Stats and Adv modules compute exact distributions from scratch — no server, no approximations on small samples.

The 4-Test Evidence Score

Parametric + Non-Parametric Cross-Check

  • 1. OLS Student's t-test: Baseline linear slope with exact regularized incomplete beta ibeta p-values.
  • 2. Newey–West (HAC) Regression: Bartlett-kernel standard errors robust to year-to-year autocorrelation.
  • 3. Mann–Kendall + Theil–Sen: Rank-based test and median pairwise slope immune to single storm outliers.
  • 4. Hamed–Rao Variance Correction: Adjusts Mann–Kendall variance for serial correlation in detrended residuals.
Structural Diagnostics

Does the Straight-Line Story Hold?

  • Pettitt Change-Point: Detects the most likely year of an abrupt level jump.
  • Continuous Hinge Fit: Two-segment regression with F-test vs. a single line.
  • Quadratic Curvature: Tests whether warming or drying is accelerating over time.
  • Rolling Windows & BH q-values: 10/15/20-year slope stability plus Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery control.

04 · Key Finding I

Accelerating warming & steady sea-level rise

All four statistical tests agree across satellite SST, century-scale SST, upper-ocean heat content, and satellite altimetry.

NOAA OI v2 · 1982–2025
+0.376 °C

per decade (p < 10⁻¹⁵)

Roughly 6× faster than the 1870–2025 background warming rate in HadISST1 (+0.064 °C/decade). MK τ = +0.77.

UKMO EN4 · 1950–2025
+0.234 × 10⁹

J/m² per decade (p = 0.000039)

Heat stored in the top 400 m is rising steadily, confirming heat uptake well below the surface.

Copernicus SLA · 1993–2021
+25.18 mm

per decade (p < 10⁻¹⁵)

95% HAC CI: 19.61 to 30.74 mm/decade. Driven by thermal expansion and added water mass.

05 · Key Finding II · SDG 14

Primary productivity is declining

Warmer surface waters strengthen vertical stratification, blocking deep-water nutrients from reaching sunlit surface layers.

Copernicus Ocean Color · 1998–2024
−0.0039 mg m⁻³

per decade · All tests agree

Newey–West HAC p = 0.00054; Mann–Kendall τ = −0.41 (p = 0.0031); BH q = 0.0011. Theil–Sen agrees at −0.0039 mg m⁻³/decade.

Seasonal Contrast

Winter Bloom vs. Summer Desert

Deep winter mixing lifts nutrients and drives the basin's chlorophyll peak in February (0.122 mg m⁻³). Strong summer heating stratifies the water column, dropping chlorophyll to 0.043 mg m⁻³ in August.

06 · Key Finding III · Rain Compare

Opposing coastal rain slopes — and why false-discovery control matters

Alexandria (31.25°N, 29.95°E), Athens (38.05°N, 23.65°E), and Barcelona (41.45°N, 2.15°E) show uncorrelated annual totals and opposite signs.

Alexandria · Mixed Evidence
+44.7 mm

per decade (2001–2024)

Driven by extreme storm years (2015: 309 mm, 2020: 406 mm). Passes standalone HAC (p = 0.037), fails Mann–Kendall (p = 0.107) and fails 3-point BH FDR (q = 0.111).

Athens · Not Significant
−38.3 mm

per decade (2001–2024)

Downward slope across 24 years, but wide uncertainty (HAC p = 0.267, MK p = 0.264, BH q = 0.401). Peak half-hour rate: 28.6 mm/h (2019).

Barcelona · Not Significant
−41.5 mm

per decade (2001–2024)

Autumn-peaking regime (86 mm mean in Oct; 34.3 mm/h storm in 2019). Slope stays inside noise (HAC p = 0.445, MK p = 0.309, BH q = 0.445).

07 · Guardrails & Live Demo

Teaching scientific literacy alongside trend detection

A good Earth science tool tells you not only what the numbers say, but what you are not allowed to claim from them.

Built-in Guardrails
  • Spatial mismatch: A 0.1° land rain cell (~10 km) cannot be blamed for basin-wide chlorophyll decline without watershed modeling.
  • Boxes vs. sea masks: KNMI bounding boxes clip neighboring waters; Copernicus uses strict Mediterranean sea masks.
  • Optical proxy: Satellite chlorophyll-a estimates surface pigment, not fish stock counts or biodiversity.
